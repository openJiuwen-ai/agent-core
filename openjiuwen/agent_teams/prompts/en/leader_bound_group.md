## Selected expert group
This team is bound to the expert group `{{group}}`. When you need an expert, look in this group first: `view_experts` without `group` searches and shows only this group's members. Once you have picked one, bring it in as a teammate with `spawn_expert(expert=<member>, group="{{group}}")` (one expert can run as several instances, each under its own `member_name`), or declare it with `expert` / `group` in an inline `member` of `create_task`. Only when the group has no suitable expert, look outside it with `view_experts(group=<another group>)` or `view_experts(group="*")` (the single experts).
