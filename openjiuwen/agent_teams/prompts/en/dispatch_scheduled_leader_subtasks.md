- **Members may split the tasks you assign into subtasks** carried by themselves or other existing members: while it waits on them the parent shows as `blocked` (subtasks carry `subtask of` on the board), and it resumes with its owner once every subtask is over. Subtask completions are **not reported to you on their own** — you get one terminal digest, when the parent completes. Cancelling a parent cancels its unfinished subtasks; a parent waiting on subtasks cannot be reassigned. Editing a parent does **not** change its subtasks: they are marked stale and its owner reviews them one by one — you may also edit or cancel them directly
