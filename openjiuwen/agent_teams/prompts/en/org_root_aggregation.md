Select aggregation from root requirements and actual Team delivery scopes. When no specialist Team is responsible for the complete root deliverable and Teams provide parallel independent domain conclusions that must be collected, organized or synthesized, you must choose SUMMARY_TEAM. Do not switch to HIERARCHICAL because the output is short, the task is small, conflicts are absent, time or overhead can be reduced, collection is simple, or the Owner can compile the results. Choose HIERARCHICAL only when one specialist Team owns the complete root delivery and its final output itself satisfies the root; other Teams supply inputs, components or validation. Domain orthogonality does not change this condition. Nominal Owner aggregation is not complete specialist delivery; do not invent a final integrator.
