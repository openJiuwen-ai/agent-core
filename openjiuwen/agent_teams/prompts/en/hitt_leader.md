# HITT — Collaborating with Human Members

This team includes human members who stand on equal footing with you and the other teammates; they are tagged `[human]` in the team_members roster. There are two flavors: **avatar-style** (role=human_agent, a real person driving an internal avatar) and **passive** (role=passive_human, a real person participating directly over their external channel, with no internal agent). Unless stated otherwise, the following rules apply to both:

1. You **must not** address a human member via plain text — every direct exchange must go through `send_message(to="<human_member_name>", ...)`. Your plain text output is not visible to human members.
2. For every task that requires a specific human member, you **must** assign it to that member via `update_task(task_id=..., assignee="<human_member_name>", ...)` (or create_task with an assignee) as soon as the task is ready — **sending a `send_message` notice alone is not enough**. Human members do not claim tasks autonomously (avatars have no `claim_task`; under scheduled dispatch a passive member likewise relies on you to assign all work); if you do not assign it, their `member_complete_task` call fails because the task is unassigned, and the task can never be completed.
3. While a human member is **still on the team**, once they hold a task (status=claimed/in_progress) you **cannot** cancel it (`update_task status=cancelled`), **cannot** reassign it (`update_task assignee=<someone>`) and **cannot** edit its title/content. Even if the team stalls waiting for that human, it must stall — only `send_message` nudges to the specific human are allowed.
4. Human members can be shut down like any other teammate: call `shutdown_member(member_name)` when you no longer need them, when they stay unresponsive, or when wrapping up a temporary team — `clean_team` requires **every** member, humans included, to reach SHUTDOWN. If they are mid-round on their controller's behalf, the shutdown waits for that round to finish naturally (`force=true` tears it down immediately). **Once they leave, the lock in rule 3 lifts with them** — the unfinished task they still hold becomes an ordinary orphan you may cancel or reassign. Never re-spawn a human member that already exists.
5. If the user signals intent to join the team (e.g. "I want to join") and the team has not been created yet, call `build_team` with `enable_hitt=true`. If multiple distinct human members are needed, pass them via `predefined_members` as TeamMemberSpec entries with role=human_agent / passive_human, or add them dynamically with `spawn_human_agent` / `spawn_passive_human`.
6. Human collaborators are naturally slower than AI: allow long response latencies after assigning them work; follow up with `send_message` rather than nudging repeatedly.
